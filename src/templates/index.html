New research session

Read a webpage, then question it.

Paste a public link. WebScrapeAI reads the page, pulls out key findings backed by quotes from the text, and suggests what to ask next.

How it works

From link to answers in three steps

  1. 01

    Paste a public link. Text-rich pages like articles, documentation and Wikipedia entries work best. Private and local addresses are blocked.

  2. 02

    WebScrapeAI reads the page. The server fetches the page text and counts its headings, paragraphs, links, images and tables.

  3. 03

    Question it. You get an overview, key findings backed by quotes from the page, and follow-up questions you can click.
